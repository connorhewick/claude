# Online maturity ladder and capability checklist

| Level | Description | Typical goal of a pass |
|---|---|---|
| L0 No presence | Offline or social/DM only | Minimum viable shop |
| L1 Listed | Website/listings; enquiry by form or phone | Self-serve checkout |
| L2 Transacting | One channel taking payment online | Reliability, conversion, fulfilment accuracy |
| L3 Multi-channel | Own site + marketplaces/feeds | Single stock and price truth, channel sync |
| L4 Optimised | Data-driven merchandising, retention, B2B, international | Personalisation, subscriptions, advanced pricing, scale |

## Capability checklist (design-phase menu — pull only what unlocks the next rung)

- **Presence & discovery:** domain/hosting, mobile-first storefront, SEO basics (titles,
  structured data, sitemaps), Google Merchant/social feeds, marketplace listings.
- **Offer content:** product data standards, images, variants, categories, search and filters.
- **Checkout & payments:** guest checkout, wallets/local payment methods, tax, shipping-rate
  rules, fraud screening, hosted payment fields (out of PCI scope).
- **Fulfilment promise:** stock reservation, delivery estimates, carrier integration, labels,
  order-status emails. *Digital/service offers:* entitlement delivery (access, licence key,
  booking confirmation), recurring billing, dunning.
- **Customer lifecycle:** accounts, order tracking, returns/refunds, reviews, email/SMS flows,
  abandoned-cart recovery.
- **Trust & compliance:** policies, consent (GDPR), accessibility, consumer-rights rules,
  secure-checkout signals.
- **Analytics & operations:** funnel events, dashboards, alerts on checkout failure, admin
  tooling.
